# WinMonitorVolume Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

WinMonitorVolume is published by Little Feet Services Pvt Ltd. This policy covers the WinMonitorVolume app for Windows, including the version distributed through the Microsoft Store.

## Summary

WinMonitorVolume does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

## What the app does

The app sets the speaker volume of your external monitor. It sends DDC/CI commands to the monitor through the Windows Monitor Configuration API (`dxva2.dll`). All of this happens on your own computer.

## Information we collect

None. The app:

- has no user accounts and does not ask for any personal information;
- makes no network connections, and has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising;
- does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

## Information stored on your device

If you choose a "When I sign in to Windows" option, the app saves that choice on your computer: a short setting such as `--tray` or `--set 50`. The Microsoft Store version saves it in a small file in your local app data folder. The standalone version saves it in your Windows sign-in startup entry. This setting never leaves your computer. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version deletes it. In the standalone version, choosing "Don't start automatically" in the app removes it.

## Links

The About window contains links to websites (mangoidiots.com and GitHub). They open in your web browser only when you click them. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
